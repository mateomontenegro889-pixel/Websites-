# Ravara Ad #7: "Fun fact" (owner-focused)

Image: `assets/ravara-ig7-funfact.png` (1:1). Source: `ads/build7.py`, then `node ads/render7.js`.

## Why this fact

"Fun fact" posts usually lead with a statistic. The brief bans unsourced stats, so this uses a fact that is
simply true for every owner: lost jobs are invisible. Jobs booked and invoices are tracked; jobs lost to
missed calls aren't tracked anywhere. That's the realisation the ad needs him to have.

## Primary text

Fun fact: the jobs you lose never show up on your books.
No invoice. No line item. Just a missed call while you were on the tools.
That caller didn't leave a voicemail. They called the next company on the list.

Now every missed call gets a text back in seconds, from your business, in your name.
They reply while you finish the job, so it stays your job.

No app to learn. No new number. Nothing for you to do.
Calgary, Airdrie, Okotoks, High River, Chestermere and Cochrane HVAC and plumbing.
Book a free call.

- **Headline:** The jobs you lose never show up on your books.
- **Description:** Calgary HVAC and plumbing
- **CTA button:** Book Now
- **Organic version:** end with "How many missed calls did you have last week? Be honest." to drive comments.
