# Ravara Ad #6: direct-response (owner-focused)

Image: `assets/ravara-ig6-direct.png` (1:1). Source: `ads/build6.py`, then `node ads/render6.js`.

## Why this layout converts

1. **Callout bar:** "CALGARY HVAC & PLUMBING OWNERS". The right person knows in half a second it's for him; everyone else scrolls past (cheaper clicks).
2. **Headline names the loss in his words:** "Stop losing jobs to the next company on the list."
3. **The mechanism in 3 steps:** he understands the whole product without reading a caption.
4. **Objections removed:** no app, no new number, nothing to do.
5. **A real reason to act now:** "Get set up before the first cold snap." This is true urgency, not a fake deadline.
6. **One CTA:** Book a free call.
