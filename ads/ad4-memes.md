# Ravara Ad #4: meme-style ads (hi-vis look)

Images: `assets/ravara-ig4-meme-a.png` (text-post meme), `assets/ravara-ig4-meme-b.png` (POV without vs. with).
Source: `ads/build4.py`, then `node ads/render4.js`. Both are 1:1, 3240px.

## Why a meme

- Every competitor ad (AI receptionists, Jobber/HCP, answering services) looks like software
  marketing: navy and blue, dashboards, stat walls. A hi-vis yellow meme doesn't look like an ad,
  so people stop on it and read it.
- Memes get shared and tagged ("this is literally you @dave"). For a trade audience, that's free reach
  to other owners.
- The joke is the problem. Nobody needs the product explained. They recognise the moment, laugh,
  and feel it.
- Hi-vis yellow and black hazard stripes look like the job site, not tech.

## Meme A: text post

Homeowner at -30: calls your business.
You: under a sink in Okotoks.
Homeowner, immediately: calls the next company on the list.

Unless your line texts them back first, from your business, in your name.
They reply while you finish the job. The lead is waiting when your hands are free.

No app. No new number. No robot on your phone.
For HVAC and plumbing in Calgary, Airdrie, Okotoks, High River, Chestermere and Cochrane.
Book a free call.

**Headline:** Missed a call? Still book the job.

## Meme B: POV without vs. with

**Primary text**

POV: you finally crawl out from under the sink and check your phone.
Left: one missed call, no voicemail. That job went to the next guy.
Right: same missed call, but they already texted back: "Furnace quit, house is down to 12."

Every missed call gets a text back from your business, in your name.
No app to learn. No new number. Nothing for you to do.
One recovered call pays for the month.

Calgary-area HVAC and plumbing. Book a free call.

**Headline:** Same missed call. Only one is still a job.

## Both

- **Description:** Calgary HVAC and plumbing
- **CTA button:** Book Now
- **Organic post:** end with "Tag a tradesman who's always under a sink" (drives comments and shares), then "Book a free call. Link in bio."
- **Test:** run A and B in the same ad set as the carousel for 7 days at equal budget. Judge on cost per booked call.
